## Market Insights

- The market shows a high demand for specialized intern/co-op roles, particularly in robotics and software engineering.
- Python, SQL, and Java are foundational requirements across various experience levels.
- Cloud proficiency (AWS) and containerization (Docker/Kubernetes) are highly valued preferred skills.
- Salary transparency varies significantly, with some postings providing wide ranges and others providing no information.
